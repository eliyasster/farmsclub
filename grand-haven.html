About the farm

Grand Haven is the largest farm in the club — twelve acres of date palms and lawn on the Kalba side of the mountains, where the air comes off the Gulf of Oman. The house is low and thick-walled: eight bedrooms, a majlis that seats sixty, and doors that open straight onto grass.

Behind the palms is the working half of the farm: the goat and sheep yard, a chicken run, two horses and a camel with a handler who takes children out at sunset. Feeding is at 7am and 5pm, and it is the part every guest remembers.

Evenings happen at the barbecue yard — two charcoal grills under a covered deck, long tables, and string lights that go on at seven. Music down by ten; the neighbours are farms too.

The last stretch. Tarmac to the gate. No 4×4 needed — the last 600 m is a graded farm track, flat and fine for a saloon car. Gated parking for 20 cars inside the wall.

Arrival. We send the pin, the gate code and the caretaker’s number on WhatsApp the morning of your stay, and again an hour before check-in. Nobody waits at a locked gate.

Phone and Wi-Fi. Etisalat and du both full strength across the plot. Wi-Fi reaches the majlis, the bedrooms and about half the lawn.

Between every stay

Every room is cleaned before you arrive and again after you leave — not turned over between two groups on the same day. The farm is managed by one team across all three properties, so the standard does not depend on which caretaker is on.

  • Bedrooms and bathrooms cleaned before check-in and after check-out
  • Fresh linen and towels for every booking
  • Pool tested and treated before each arrival
  • Kitchen, majlis and barbecue yard cleared down between stays
  • Caretaker on the plot, reachable and out of the way

Grand Haven FAQs

Guests, rooms, pets and pools — the answers that differ farm to farm. General booking and payment questions live on the club FAQs.

Ask the farm manager
How many people can stay at Grand Haven?+

Eight air-conditioned bedrooms with 18 beds, so 18 guests overnight. For day bookings the farm takes 60 — the majlis seats 60 and the lawn holds more.

How many bedrooms and bathrooms are there?+

Eight bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, plus two guest bathrooms off the majlis and a changing room by the pool.

Can we bring our own pets?+

No. Grand Haven keeps goats, sheep, horses and a camel loose in parts of the plot, so guests’ animals have to stay home. Happy Haven is the pet-friendly farm in the club.

What animals are on the farm?+

Goats, sheep, chickens and rabbits in the yard, two horses, and a camel with a handler who takes children out at sunset. Feeding is at 7am and 5pm and guests are welcome to join.

Is there a pool, and is it heated?+

A heated 20m pool and a separate shaded kids’ pool. Heating runs November to March at no extra cost; say so when you book and it is on before you arrive.

What are check-in and check-out times?+

Overnight 3pm to 12pm; day bookings 10am to 10pm. Early check-in is usually fine when the farm is free the night before.

How much parking is there, and how far is Dubai?+

Gated parking for 20 cars. It is about 1h45 from Dubai and 45 minutes from Fujairah.

Can we host a wedding or a large event here?+

Grand Haven is the farm built for it — 60 seated in the majlis, a covered barbecue yard and two grills. Birthdays, corporate days and shoots need no approval; weddings and amplified stages need written approval, and music is down by 10pm.
