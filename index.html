How we run the farms

Three farms, one team. Not three separate hosts you hope reply.

The whole property

You get the gate code and the entire farm — the pool, the lawn, the majlis, the yard. No shared plots, no other group on the land.

Cleaned both ways

Every room is cleaned before you arrive and again after you leave. Fresh linen every booking, and the pool tested before each arrival.

Answered in minutes

One WhatsApp thread, 8am to 11pm, usually answered inside fifteen minutes — by the people who actually run the farm.

Nothing hidden

Drive times, road surface, gate access, pool heating months and house rules are all published on each farm page before you book.

FAQs

Anything not here, open Select any questions in the corner — there is a longer list in there, and it points you to the farm manager when a question needs a person.

How do I book a farm?+

Call or WhatsApp the farm manager with the farm, the dates and the number of guests. We hold the farm for 24 hours while you confirm, then a refundable security deposit locks it in. A self-serve availability calendar is coming to this site soon.

Why are prices not on the site?+

Rates move with the farm, the season and whether it is a day booking or an overnight stay. Everything else you need is on these pages — so when you call, the only thing left to discuss is the number.

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Overnight stays run 3pm to 12pm. Day bookings run 10am to 10pm. Early check-in is often possible when the farm is free the night before.

Is the whole farm ours?+

Yes — the house, the pools, the lawns, the barbecue yard and the animal area are yours for the booking. Only the caretaker stays on site, and only in the service quarters.

Can we bring pets?+

At Happy Haven, yes, on request — it is the pet-friendly farm. The other two keep livestock loose in parts of the plot, so guests' animals stay home.

Can we host an event?+

Birthdays, family gatherings, corporate days and shoots are welcome. Amplified music must be down by 10pm, and weddings need written approval first.

What are the house rules?+

No smoking indoors, no fireworks, no alcohol on the farms, no unregistered overnight guests, and music down by 10pm out of respect for neighbouring farms.
